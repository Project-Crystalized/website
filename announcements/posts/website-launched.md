## What's on the site?

Some parts of the site are still pretty barebones and will be developed more in the future.
Here is the current planned layout:
-  **Home**  Server info and a quick overview of what Crystalized is all about
-  **Announcements**  News and updates (like this one!)
-  **Our Games** Details, explanations, plans and showcasing of all our minigames.
-  **Downloads** Quickly grab resourcepacks, maps for all the games, instructions on config files etc.
