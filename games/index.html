Our Games

  • Litestrike

    The game concept is similar to Counter Strike or Valorant. There are two teams that are fighting against each other in a Map.
    One of the teams, the placers, gets the a bomb called the SHARD. That team needs to bring the SHARD to one of a few specific places on the map, and plant it there, and defend the SHARD for 40 seconds, then they have won. They can also win by killing the entire enemy team.
    The Other team, the breakers, have to prevent the SHARD from being planted, or defuse the SHARD after it has been planted.
